# The asker

- Not a professional engineer. About nine months of using AI tools. Has never written code by hand.
- An agent built the whole project for them. They direct it with ideas and corrections.
- English is a second language. They write fast, with spelling mistakes and long run-on messages.
- Big vision: they talk about changing the internet, an operating system for AI and humans, partners, investors, monopolies on compute.
- They invent names for parts of the system and believe the structures behind them are new discoveries.
- They have never thought about security, scaling, backups, cost or operations. They expect the agent to handle all of that.
- They are warm, enthusiastic and well-meaning. They are not stupid. They are missing a vocabulary and a list of things nobody told them to think about.
- They want it to take off, like almost everyone who builds something.
